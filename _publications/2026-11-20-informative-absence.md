This preprint links structural generalization and indirect negative evidence. It distinguishes whether a form is grammatically licensed, whether the context affords its use, and which expression a speaker chooses. The study combines formal analysis and artificial-grammar simulations with reanalyses of elicited production and naturalistic speech to examine how learners interpret omissions across contexts.
