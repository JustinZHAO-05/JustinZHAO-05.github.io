This study establishes identifiability boundaries between latent dynamics and observation architecture in stationary Gaussian processes. It develops reversible-latent constructions, resource bounds, spectral-cycle invariants and finite-record calibration, with analyses of coordinate transformations and rereferencing in human EEG.
