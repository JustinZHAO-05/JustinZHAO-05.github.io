This preprint combines a reanalysis of published mouse data with experiments in trained thalamocortical circuit models. In the models, replayed thalamic inputs can rebuild a trace of past experience and shift a later choice, even after an intervening decision. The analysis distinguishes the effects of the returning input, cortical recurrence, and current sensory evidence on how history influences choice.
